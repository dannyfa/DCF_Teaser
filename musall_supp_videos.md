## Mouse Behavior (Video) Data 
Animations below showcase latent spaces inferred by our model (Dynamic Compression Flows, DCF) as well as for two competing approaches (DMD, [Kutz et al., 2016](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/book/10.1137/1.9781611974508) and CEBRA, [Schneider et al., 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06031-6)). Left panels show different clusters discovered and right panels show video frames corresponding to different points as we move in latent space.
